FINDING 1 — the probe's type-mix factor was a HARDCODED BRACKET TABLE, and it is exact.
  v0.1.74's `MIX_FACTOR` / `MIX_MAX_WAVE` consts are a wave-bracketed E[hp_mult] table copied out
  of map_difficulty.md §1.2; they cannot see the probation schedule. This task added a live-derived
  mix — the weight-weighted mean `hp_mult` over exactly the rows `Enemies.pick()` would consider
  on that wave, using `Enemies.min_wave_for(row, probation)` for eligibility and the same integer
  `weight` values it rolls against (hive broods excluded: `pick()` never returns one) — and applied
  it to BOTH columns of the new section. Measured gap against the old bracket table at the sampled
  waves: worst 0.043% (w4: live 1.1355 vs table 1.135). The bracket table is a rounded snapshot of
  exactly this computation, so no drift, nothing to reconcile, and the v0.1.74 rows are unaffected.

FINDING 2 — a real defect in the probation feature, FIXED IN THIS RELEASE ("training pay").
  The first run of this section measured the probation column arriving at 4:00 — the minute the two
  schedules CONVERGE (probation threat ÷ normal threat = 1.000, identical pools) — at level 13
  against a normal save's 14. Cause: a gentler opening drops fewer and lower-value gems (mean gem
  value per spawn 1.00 vs 1.48 at 1:30, 2.29 vs 3.56 at 3:00), so probation was buying its safety
  with roughly one whole gun card (~×1.28 DPS at that stage) — handed over at precisely the moment
  the game stops being gentle. It also ate a third of the first boss's softening (51.8 s instead of
  the 40.7 s that ×0.60 implies).
  Fixed by `GameConfig.PROBATION_XP_BONUS := 0.15`, granted run-long in `Main._ready` through
  `player.upgrade_xp_gain` (the same multiplicative `xp_mult` channel NIGHT SCHOOL and the Fast
  Learner card use) whenever `RunConfig.probation`. 0.15 is the smallest 0.05 step that reaches
  level parity at 4:00 and holds power÷threat at ≥ 0.95× the normal row at all six sampled minutes;
  the whole 0.00–0.35 sweep is printed in section 4 and the probe asserts the shipped constant IS
  that smallest passing candidate, so the constant and the tuning cannot drift apart.

FINDING 3 — the remaining 2:00 deviation is a limit of the THREAT PROXY, and is tolerated.
  Threat in this model is "HP to clear per second", so each enemy row contributes its `hp_mult`.
  The exploder is `hp_mult` 0.80 — one of only two sub-1.0 rows — so it *dilutes* the mean.
  Delaying it to wave 7 while the brute (`hp_mult` 4.0) still arrives at wave 5 leaves probation's
  wave-5 pool with a **higher** mean HP than normal's: 1.1355 vs 1.0971, +3.5%. The proxy scores an
  exploder as *less* threat; in the hand it is a discrete blast (spec §3.1) this model does not
  price at all. Wave 5 is also the first boss wave, and that boss — the wave's dominant threat — is
  softened ×0.60 and is not in the trash threat number at all. The §4 check therefore requires
  ≥ 0.95× the normal row rather than ≥ 1.00×; measured worst is 0.97× at 2:00.
