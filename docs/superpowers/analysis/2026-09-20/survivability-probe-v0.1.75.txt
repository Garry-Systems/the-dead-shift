FINDING 1 — the probe's type-mix factor was a HARDCODED BRACKET TABLE, and it is exact.
  v0.1.74's `MIX_FACTOR` / `MIX_MAX_WAVE` consts are a wave-bracketed E[hp_mult] table copied out
  of map_difficulty.md §1.2; they cannot see the probation schedule. This task added a live-derived
  mix — the weight-weighted mean `hp_mult` over exactly the rows `Enemies.pick()` would consider
  on that wave, using `Enemies.min_wave_for(row, probation)` for eligibility and the same integer
  `weight` values it rolls against (hive broods excluded: `pick()` never returns one) — and applied
  it to BOTH columns of the new section. Measured gap against the old bracket table at the sampled
  waves: worst 0.043% (w4: live 1.1355 vs table 1.135). The bracket table is a rounded snapshot of
  exactly this computation, so no drift, nothing to reconcile, and the v0.1.74 rows are unaffected.

FINDING 2 — the v0.1.75 §4 probation row is NOT met at 2:00 and 4:00. Two separate causes.
  (a) 2:00 / wave 5 — a limit of the THREAT PROXY, not of the shipped schedule. Threat here is
      "HP to clear per second", so a row contributes its `hp_mult`. The exploder is hp_mult 0.80,
      one of only two sub-1.0 rows, so it DILUTES the mean. Delaying it to wave 7 while the brute
      (hp_mult 4.0) still arrives at wave 5 leaves probation's wave-5 pool with a HIGHER mean HP
      than normal's: 1.1355 vs 1.0971, +3.5%. The proxy scores an exploder as *less* threat; in
      the hand it is a discrete blast (spec §3.1) this model does not price at all. Wave 5 is also
      the first boss wave and that boss — the wave's dominant threat — is softened ×0.60 and is
      not in the trash threat number at all.
  (b) 4:00 / wave 9 — a REAL cost of the shipped schedule. By 4:00 the two schedules have already
      converged (probation threat ÷ normal threat = 1.000, identical pools) yet the probation
      column is level 13 against 14. A lighter early game pays less XP: mean gem value per spawn
      1.00 vs 1.48 at 1:30 and 2.29 vs 3.56 at 3:00. Probation buys safety with roughly one level
      by 4:00, and a FRESH level is ~×1.28 DPS at that stage, so one level is the whole gap. The
      same lag is what turns the first boss's ×0.60 into a 51.8 s fight instead of 40.7 s.
  Neither is being retuned in v0.1.75. (b) in particular is a design question for Larry, and the
  right instrument for it is a phone, not this simulation.
