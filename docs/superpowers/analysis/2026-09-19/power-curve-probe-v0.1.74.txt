"roster mean 40-70s / every boss 30-80s". The original is unreachable with one BOSS_BASE_HP + one

and every one of the 44 per-boss cells between 30.5s (w10 Night Stocker) and 79.1s (w5 Manager).

THE CARD-PICK POLICY IS DERIVED, NOT WRITTEN (review fix round 1). The first probe build hand-wrote
a 5-card cycle that included Extra Barrel — a card neither reference weapon offers — which inflated
the whole power side. The committed probe replays the real offer instead: the live pool for the
level's parity, shuffled, 3 offered, each rolling its own tier through CardRolls.roll, 200k fixed-
seed offers per pool, then a stated priority (damage > fire rate > reload > anything else; the
bigger headline gain when both DPS cards are offered). It ASSERTS that every id it can pick is in
Weapons.upgrades_for(<build weapon>) and checks its own offer rates against the hypergeometric.
